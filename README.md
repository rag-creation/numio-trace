## 🤔 What is Trace for?

We all forget small things like these:

- When did I last **change the bedsheets**?
- When did I **clean the AC filter**?
- When did I **give my laptop for repair**?
- When did I last **get a haircut**, **wash the car**, **change the water filter**?

They're too small for a calendar and too boring for a diary, so we forget them.
People end up typing them into a notes app and then scrolling and counting days.

**Trace does the remembering and the counting for you.**

## ⚡ How it works

1. **Did something?** Tap **+**, type it (`Changed AC filter`), tap **Save**. That's it, about 3 seconds.
2. **Did it again later?** Start typing and Trace suggests it, or open it and tap **Did it again**.
3. **Want to know?** Search *"AC filter"* and Trace tells you:

```
Changed AC filter
  Last time      Today
  Usually        every ~3 months
  Next, roughly  23 Feb
  Times          4

  22 Nov 2026   ·  94 days later
  20 Aug 2026   ·  94 days later
  18 May 2026   ·  92 days later
  15 Feb 2026
```

You never set a reminder or a schedule. Trace learns how often you do things from your own history.

## ✨ Features

- ✅ **Quick add**: tap +, type, save. Things you've logged before show up as you type
- ✅ **Now, yesterday or any past day**, with an optional note and tag (Home, Car, Bike…)
- ✅ **Timeline** of everything you've traced, day by day
- ✅ **"When did I last…" search** by name, tag or note
- ✅ **History of each thing**: every time you did it, the gap between each time, how often you usually do it, and roughly when it's due next
- ✅ **Coming round again**: things you do regularly, sorted by which is due first
- ✅ **Did it again** in one tap, with Undo
- ✅ **Edit or delete** any trace: long-press it on the timeline, or tap it in its history
- ✅ Light, dark or follow-system theme

## 💡 Tips

- **Use the same name every time** (e.g. always `Changed AC filter`). That's how Trace connects them and works out how often you do it.
- After **3 times**, a thing shows up in **Coming round again**.
- Trace is for things you **already did**. Future dates, like when a card or ID expires, are a job for another app.

## 🔒 Privacy

- ✅ **No permissions at all.** Not even internet. Nothing you trace ever leaves your phone.
- ✅ **No ads, no accounts, no tracking.** Ever.
- ✅ **Your data is safe on your phone.** Saving writes a new copy first and only then swaps it in, and the previous copy is kept as a backup, so a crash or a dead battery never leaves you with half-saved data.
- ✅ Every build is checked automatically to make sure the internet permission never sneaks in.
